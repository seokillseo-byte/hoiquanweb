## 5. Brand messaging rules

1. Use the approved factual business description verbatim where a full description is needed.
2. Preserve all seven service groups without merging or dropping them.
3. Keep B2C first in initial content emphasis and retain a route for B2B.
4. Use consultation/quotation as the primary CTA.
5. Do not publish a USP, comparative claim, guarantee, unverified history, customer count, testimonial, logo, certification, award, project result or SLA.
6. Vietnamese and English content must be natural, equivalent in meaning and independently reviewed.
7. Do not use AI-generated visuals to imply actual staff, clients, offices or completed work.
