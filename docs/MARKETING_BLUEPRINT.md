- The relevant channel and analytics source are configured lawfully.
- A baseline period is available.
- Inquiry qualification criteria are defined.
- The Project Owner approves the target and reporting period.

## 5. Channel strategy framework

No channel budget or active campaign is authorized by this document.

| Channel | Intended role | Initial suitability hypothesis | Prerequisites / risks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic search (SEO) | Capture informational and service-intent demand over time | Strong strategic fit with SEO-first website direction | Keyword evidence, content quality, technical SEO, time horizon; no ranking guarantee |
| Website content / knowledge | Educate and route visitors to relevant services | Supports seven service hubs and bilingual discovery | Approved service scope, editorial review, localization |
| Social organic | Build awareness and distribute useful content | Potential supporting channel | Audience/channel fit, content capacity, brand account ownership |
| Paid search | Test demand for specific services and geographies | Consider only after landing pages and inquiry measurement work | Budget approval, keyword economics, conversion tracking, policy review |
| Paid social | Reach defined audience segments and retarget where lawful | Defer until audience and creative are validated | Consent/privacy, platform policies, budget and measurement |
| Partnerships / referrals | Reach relevant customers through credible relationships | Potential channel after service scope is clear | Partner criteria, disclosure, referral terms and operational capacity |
| Email | Follow up with inquiries and permission-based updates | Operational follow-up only after consent and sender readiness | Privacy/consent, deliverability, list provenance and unsubscribe handling |
| Communities / events | Build subject-matter visibility and relationships | Explore only where target audience participation is evidenced | Relevance, moderation rules, time and event costs |

Do not launch paid campaigns before the destination pages, privacy controls, inquiry flow and measurement are accepted.

## 6. Funnel and customer journey

| Stage | Visitor need | Marketing/content asset | Desired next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Understand a technology need or possible solution | Useful informational content and factual brand introduction | Explore a relevant service |
| Consideration | Understand service scope and evaluate fit | Service overview/detail pages and verified evidence | Ask a question / request consultation |
| Conversion | Explain requirement and receive a response | Contact / quotation flow | Submit inquiry; count only server acceptance |
| Follow-up | Clarify requirements and quotation | Owner-managed communication | Continue the real inquiry process |
| Retention / referral | Seek further work or recommend service | Only approved follow-up and relationship practices | Repeat inquiry/referral when appropriate |

No automated nurture sequence, response-time commitment or CRM workflow is assumed.

## 7. Campaign and content framework

### 7.1 Initial sequence

1. Complete factual VI/EN homepage, About, Services and Contact foundations.
2. Prepare seven service briefs and obtain confirmed scope/deliverable inputs.
3. Validate keyword research and search intent by language/market.
4. Publish reviewed supporting content only where it answers a distinct user need.
5. Establish analytics and inquiry measurement with privacy approval.
6. Run a small, explicitly approved channel test only after the relevant prerequisites pass.
7. Review results and obtain approval before scaling.

### 7.2 Campaign brief template

Every future campaign must specify:
- Campaign ID, owner and approval date.
- Business objective and target audience.
- Market, language and channel.
- Offer/message, supported by evidence.
- Destination URL and page readiness.
- Budget and spending cap.
- Start/end dates and operational capacity.
- Tracking, consent and attribution approach.
- Primary KPI, baseline and target.
- Risks, stop conditions and reporting owner.

No campaign may be launched from an incomplete brief.

## 8. Conversion and lead handling

- Primary CTA: consultation / quotation.
- The approved inquiry notification destination is `info@hoiquanweb.io.vn`; the Project Owner is the stated handler. Mailbox access and delivery remain unverified.
- Do not present a form as live until privacy/consent wording, configured API, sender, durable storage and end-to-end notification QA are accepted.
- A success state must reflect server acceptance, not a front-end click.
- Retention policy: 24 months from last interaction; reset only when the Project Owner sends a quotation or the customer replies; delete personal information and inquiry/request content at expiry; backups follow normal lifecycle and expired records must be removed after restore before normal operation. Exact backup lifecycle duration and final legal text remain open.
- Do not promise response times, free consultation, free quotation, or other terms unless explicitly approved.

## 10. Marketing KPIs and reporting

No numeric targets are approved. Establish data sources and baselines first.

| KPI | Definition | Source / condition |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified inquiries | Inquiries meeting Owner-approved qualification criteria and durably accepted by backend | Inquiry system, only after runtime acceptance |
| Organic visibility | Search impressions, clicks, CTR and average position where available | Search Console or equivalent, if connected |
| Qualified organic traffic | Organic sessions to relevant approved service/content pages | Privacy-approved analytics |
| Landing-page engagement | Meaningful engagement on approved landing pages | Analytics definitions must be documented |
| Conversion rate | Qualified server-accepted inquiries divided by an agreed eligible visitor/session denominator | Requires reliable analytics + inquiry event linkage |
| Paid channel efficiency | Spend and qualified inquiries by campaign | Only after approved budget and attribution |
| Content effectiveness | Content-assisted service discovery and qualified inquiry contribution | Analytics and attribution methodology |
| Operational quality | Inquiry delivery failures, response handling and unresolved lead backlog | Owner-approved operational tracking |

Report data coverage, attribution limitations and missing sources. Do not compare channels using inconsistent denominators.

## 12. Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Unsupported claims harm trust or create legal exposure | Factual-only messaging; evidence and Owner approval |
| Broad service list creates vague promises | Confirm actual scope and deliverables per service |
| Paid campaigns send traffic to incomplete pages | Landing-page, form and privacy acceptance before launch |
| Tracking overstates leads | Count only server-accepted, qualified inquiries |
| Bilingual pages are thin or mechanically translated | Native-quality localization and parity review |
| Privacy or email delivery failure | Consent, API, sender and end-to-end tests before activation |
| Unapproved spend | Owner-approved budget and stop conditions |
| No baseline leads to misleading targets | Baseline before numeric KPI commitment |
| B2B messaging is premature | Keep B2B accessible; sequence dedicated expansion after B2C baseline |
