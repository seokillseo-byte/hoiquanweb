## 1. Purpose

Define the confirmed business foundation, distinguish approved facts from unresolved business inputs, and translate those facts into bounded website requirements. This document does not invent an operating model, revenue model, target persona, service promise or commercial policy.

## 2. Business baseline

### 2.1 Approved business description

Use this exact owner-approved wording where the business description is required:

> HOIQUANWEB cung cấp dịch vụ thiết kế và phát triển website, phần mềm, ứng dụng di động, game, SEO, giải pháp AI và tự động hóa cùng các dịch vụ công nghệ liên quan. Website giới thiệu dịch vụ và tiếp nhận yêu cầu tư vấn, báo giá từ khách hàng tại Việt Nam và thị trường quốc tế.

This approval covers factual description only. It does not approve formal legal/industry classification, operating model, revenue model, USP, competitive claim or detailed personas.

## 3. Business Blueprint deliverables

The following register follows the mandatory Business Blueprint deliverables in Blueprint Standard v3.0. Each item has an ID, objective, description, priority, user/stakeholder, dependencies, acceptance criteria, implementation notes and quality checklist.

### BP-BIZ-01 — Business Goals

- **Objective:** Define the business outcomes the website is intended to support.
- **Description:** The confirmed website-level goal is to introduce the seven service groups and receive consultation/quotation requests from Vietnam and international visitors. Numeric revenue, lead, growth or market-share goals have not been supplied.
- **Priority:** P0.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; prospective B2C and B2B customers.
- **Dependencies:** DEC-005, DEC-006, DEC-007, DEC-011; inquiry workflow acceptance.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Goal is traceable to Owner decisions; website conversion is distinguished from company revenue; no invented numeric target.
- **Implementation notes:** Use service discovery and consultation/quotation as the public conversion direction. Define measurable targets only after baseline and Owner approval.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Goal is explicit [ ] Metric owner/source identified before targets [ ] No fabricated revenue or lead target.

### BP-BIZ-02 — Business Model

- **Objective:** Record the customer and delivery relationship without assuming how the company operates commercially.
- **Description:** Customer scope is hybrid B2C and B2B, with B2C first and B2B next. The website is lead-generation oriented. The underlying commercial/operating model is not confirmed.
- **Priority:** P0 dependency.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; individual and organizational customers.
- **Dependencies:** Owner decision D-02; Project Input 01.2.
- **Acceptance criteria:** B2C/B2B scope and sequence are stated correctly; lead-generation website conversion is not misrepresented as a confirmed revenue model.
- **Implementation notes:** Keep the public inquiry path available to both audiences. Do not publish subscription, marketplace, agency-retainer or other commercial model assumptions.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Both segments retained [ ] B2C-first sequence respected [ ] Operating model marked TBD.

### BP-BIZ-03 — Revenue Model

- **Objective:** Establish how the business earns revenue once the Owner supplies the facts.
- **Description:** Revenue model, pricing structure, billing frequency, payment terms and quotation policy are not confirmed. Consultation/quotation is the approved website conversion only.
- **Priority:** Planning prerequisite; not a blocker for neutral service descriptions.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; Finance/Operations role when assigned.
- **Dependencies:** Project Input 01.8; business operating information.
- **Acceptance criteria:** No public pricing or revenue statement without explicit Owner confirmation and supporting terms.
- **Implementation notes:** Use “request a consultation/quotation” without implying free service, fixed price, subscription or payment method.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Revenue model TBD visible [ ] No invented price [ ] No unsupported payment/contract terms.

### BP-BIZ-04 — Value Proposition

- **Objective:** Explain customer value using validated service scope and needs.
- **Description:** A future value proposition must connect a verified customer need to a confirmed deliverable and evidence. Detailed customer needs, service boundaries and proof are currently incomplete.
- **Priority:** P0 content dependency; public claim restricted.
- **User/stakeholder:** B2C first; B2B next; Project Owner.
- **Dependencies:** Customer segments/personas, service scope, Content Blueprint, evidence.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Every public value statement is factual, specific and supported; no implied advantage is invented.
- **Implementation notes:** Core Preview uses the approved factual business description and clear service explanations only.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Need is evidenced [ ] Deliverable is confirmed [ ] Claim is approved [ ] No guarantee/comparison.

### BP-BIZ-05 — USP

- **Objective:** Govern any future unique selling proposition.
- **Description:** No public USP or comparative claim is authorized for Core Preview under DEC-013. This is a publication restriction, not a claim that no differentiator exists.
- **Priority:** Restricted / deferred for Core Preview.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; prospective customers.
- **Dependencies:** Verified differentiator, competitor evidence, legal/claim review and Owner approval.
- **Acceptance criteria:** No public USP appears unless a distinct claim and its evidence are separately approved.
- **Implementation notes:** Do not use “best”, “leading”, “number one”, guaranteed results, unverified experience, customer counts or performance promises.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] DEC-013 respected [ ] Evidence retained [ ] Owner approval recorded.

### BP-BIZ-06 — Competitive Advantage

- **Objective:** Identify defensible advantages only after evidence exists.
- **Description:** No competitive advantage is currently approved. Competitor Blueprint is a research framework only; no competitor has yet been selected or assessed.
- **Priority:** Planning prerequisite.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; Research/SEO role.
- **Dependencies:** Competitor research, internal capability evidence, customer feedback.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Each advantage traces to verifiable evidence and is approved before public use.
- **Implementation notes:** Do not derive advantages from color palette, broad service list, code presence or third-party SEO estimates.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Evidence-backed [ ] Competitor comparison fair [ ] Claim approval captured.

### BP-BIZ-07 — Customer Segments

- **Objective:** Define who the website serves and the approved sequencing.
- **Description:** Both individuals (B2C) and organizations (B2B) are in scope. B2C is the first communication/UX priority; B2B follows after the B2C-first baseline is established.
- **Priority:** P0.
- **User/stakeholder:** B2C and B2B visitors; Project Owner.
- **Dependencies:** DEC-011; target-market inputs.
- **Acceptance criteria:** B2C-first is visible in homepage hierarchy; B2B is not excluded; exact subsegments remain TBD until validated.
- **Implementation notes:** Maintain a general inquiry route usable by both segments; do not invent industries or organization sizes.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Both groups represented [ ] Correct sequencing [ ] No fabricated demographics.

### BP-BIZ-08 — Customer Personas

- **Objective:** Create validated profiles to guide messaging and UX.
- **Description:** Detailed B2C personas, age groups, use cases, buying triggers and B2B buyer roles/company sizes are not supplied.
- **Priority:** Planning prerequisite for detailed marketing/content personalization.
- **User/stakeholder:** B2C first; B2B next; UX/Content teams.
- **Dependencies:** Owner input, real inquiry data and/or customer research.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Persona fields have traceable evidence and are not generated from stereotypes.
- **Implementation notes:** Use audience-level neutral journeys in Core Preview until research is available.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Evidence source [ ] Segment distinction [ ] No invented demographic facts [ ] Owner review.

### BP-BIZ-09 — User Problems

- **Objective:** Describe customer problems accurately.
- **Description:** Specific customer pain points are not yet confirmed for any of the seven service groups.
- **Priority:** P0 content dependency.
- **User/stakeholder:** Prospective customers; Project Owner.
- **Dependencies:** Interviews, inquiry analysis, approved service scope.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Public problem statements reflect validated user language or are framed neutrally as possible needs, not universal facts.
- **Implementation notes:** Do not claim that a customer has a particular problem without research.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Source recorded [ ] Segment context [ ] No universalizing assumption.

### BP-BIZ-10 — User Needs

- **Objective:** Map validated needs to services and inquiry journeys.
- **Description:** Individual and organizational needs remain to be researched; the seven service categories are confirmed but their detailed deliverables are not.
- **Priority:** P0 content/UX dependency.
- **User/stakeholder:** B2C and B2B visitors.
- **Dependencies:** BP-BIZ-07 to BP-BIZ-09; service-scope confirmation.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Each published need-to-service mapping is clear and supported by confirmed service capability.
- **Implementation notes:** Avoid asserting technologies, integrations, support or delivery terms that have not been confirmed.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Need validated [ ] Service mapping verified [ ] Scope boundaries visible.

### BP-BIZ-11 — Business Opportunities

- **Objective:** Record possible growth opportunities without presenting hypotheses as facts.
- **Description:** Potential directions include improving bilingual service discovery and building a B2C-first inquiry funnel, then expanding B2B messaging. These are planning directions based on confirmed scope, not evidence of market demand.
- **Priority:** Planning.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; Marketing/SEO.
- **Dependencies:** Market research, audience validation, channel data.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Opportunity statements are labelled hypotheses until validated; no market-size or demand claim without sources.
- **Implementation notes:** Prioritize low-risk validation through approved content and measured inquiry data.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Hypothesis labelled [ ] Evidence plan [ ] No fabricated market metrics.

### BP-BIZ-12 — Business Risks

- **Objective:** Connect business uncertainties to delivery and conversion risk.
- **Description:** Key risks include unclear service boundaries, missing target-market detail, unverified proof, unknown pricing/operating model, and a not-yet-accepted inquiry runtime.
- **Priority:** P0 governance.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; Development; QA.
- **Dependencies:** Risk Assessment; Feature Blueprint; Security and QA evidence.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Risks are linked to the project risk register and have accountable roles, mitigation and residual disposition.
- **Implementation notes:** Keep form activation blocked until privacy, API, sender, mailbox and end-to-end acceptance conditions pass.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Risk IDs cross-linked [ ] Mitigation measurable [ ] Residual status explicit.

### BP-BIZ-13 — Business Constraints

- **Objective:** Capture limits that govern scope and commitments.
- **Description:** Confirmed constraints include Core Preview P0 only, bilingual VI/EN, no unauthorized scope expansion, no public USP/comparative claims, and no merge/deployment/production change without explicit approval. Commercial, staffing, budget and legal constraints remain TBD.
- **Priority:** P0.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; all delivery roles.
- **Dependencies:** DEC-009, DEC-010, DEC-013; HDO-019 governance.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Scope and release restrictions are consistently reflected in plans, PR and communications.
- **Implementation notes:** Treat P1/P2 and platform capabilities as deferred until separately authorized.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] P0 boundary respected [ ] Approval gates documented [ ] Unknown constraints remain TBD.

### BP-BIZ-14 — Success Metrics

- **Objective:** Define measurable outcomes and valid measurement conditions.
- **Description:** Website primary conversion is consultation/quotation. Numeric targets, qualified-lead definition, analytics configuration and baseline are not approved.
- **Priority:** P0 acceptance planning.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; Marketing/Analytics; QA.
- **Dependencies:** Accepted inquiry backend, privacy approval, analytics and baseline period.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Metrics have definitions, sources, denominators and owners before numeric targets are set; only server-accepted inquiries count as received.
- **Implementation notes:** Distinguish visits, CTA clicks, form attempts, server-accepted inquiries and qualified inquiries.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] Event definitions [ ] Data source [ ] Denominator [ ] Privacy [ ] No invented target.

### BP-BIZ-15 — Future Expansion

- **Objective:** Preserve a controlled path for later business and platform growth.
- **Description:** B2B-specific journeys are the next audience expansion after the B2C-first baseline. Customer portal, advanced CMS, CRM, commerce/payment, AI chatbot/content automation and hosting/domain provisioning are outside Core Preview P0 unless separately approved.
- **Priority:** Future / deferred.
- **User/stakeholder:** Project Owner; future product/business roles.
- **Dependencies:** Core Preview acceptance, validated demand, separate requirements and authorization.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Future items remain roadmap-only and do not leak into P0 scope.
- **Implementation notes:** Track proposals in roadmap with separate priority, requirements and approval.
- **Quality checklist:** [ ] P0 separated [ ] Demand validated [ ] Separate authorization recorded.
